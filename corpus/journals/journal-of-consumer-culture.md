# Journal of Consumer Culture — review profile

## Basics

- Publisher / society: SAGE
- Region focus: International readership; strong UK/European cultural sociology tradition
- Primary disciplines: Sociology, cultural studies, consumer research, anthropology
- Typical article types: Original research articles; theoretical and empirical qualitative work common

## Aims and scope

Journal of Consumer Culture publishes work on consumption as a cultural and social
phenomenon — not only marketing metrics or individual preference models.

## Qualitative methods commonly seen

Ethnography, interviews, discourse-informed analysis, material culture approaches,
multi-site qualitative designs tied to consumption practices.

## Theory depth expected

Theoretical framing is typically **central**, not decorative — CCT, practice theory,
Bourdieu, feminist and postcolonial approaches appear frequently.

## Empirical scale and case logic

Single and comparative cases are acceptable when analytically rich; contribution must
exceed thick description.

## Structure and submission expectations

Standard social-science article structure; methods and reflexivity expected for
qualitative submissions.

## Fit signals (Final Reviewer)

**Strong fit if:**

- Consumption is analyzed as culture, practice, or meaning — not only market behavior
- Theory and empirical analysis are integrated

**Weak fit / desk-review risk if:**

- Purely quantitative market segmentation without cultural analysis
- Method label without analytic depth; purely descriptive industry report tone
